What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

A CSSBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds before purchasing through a shopping agent. Useful entries record the original listing, selected size and color, price, seller information and questions still to resolve. The list can be a downloadable sheet or a browsable web catalog.

This page combines a CAFANS product snapshot with a comparison checklist. It does not confirm that every item can be purchased through CSSBuy. Open the catalog, find the original marketplace listing, and check whether your chosen agent accepts the exact item before paying.

How to use your CSSBuy spreadsheet

Keep a clear trail from the catalog example to the exact item you intend to buy.

  1. Find and identify the item

    Open CAFANS, locate the product and save its original marketplace URL. A catalog homepage or an inspiration image does not identify the exact seller listing.

  2. Check the selected variant

    Record size, color, quantity and current price. Compare centimeter measurements with an item you already own; a size label alone is not enough.

  3. Confirm agent acceptance and costs

    Check whether CSSBuy accepts the original listing and your destination. Review current service terms, product restrictions and charges before placing an order.

  4. Review inspection information

    If warehouse photographs are provided, compare them with your selected variant. Ask about visible discrepancies and return deadlines before approving dispatch.

  5. Request the parcel quote

    Compare available shipping options for the packed parcel. Note the charged weight, included services and any charges payable later.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official CSSBuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent research page. Its catalog examples come from CAFANS, and its shopping buttons open CAFANS. No official CSSBuy affiliation is claimed.

Does “Buy it now” place an order on CSSBuy?

No. It opens the CAFANS homepage. Locate your item there, inspect the source listing and confirm your purchasing options. No payment or order is submitted by this page.

Are the displayed prices current checkout prices?

No. They are estimated USD item prices from a CAFANS catalog snapshot. A selected size, color or quantity can have a different price. Confirm the current source price and all additional charges before paying.

Can I use every product link with CSSBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified for these examples. Obtain the original marketplace listing and check current agent acceptance, seller availability and destination restrictions for the exact item.

Does a catalog photograph count as warehouse QC?

No. A seller or catalog photograph illustrates a listing. Warehouse inspection photographs, when supplied, relate to a received item. Review the exact variant and ask about visible issues; photographs alone do not certify authenticity or material composition.

Why can international shipping cost more than expected?

The final packed parcel can differ from an early estimate. Compare the route's weight calculation, dimensions, packaging, surcharges and included services. Keep shipping separate from item prices until you have a relevant quote.

Can I browse without downloading a spreadsheet?

Yes. The catalog examples are readable directly on this page. With JavaScript enabled, you can search, filter by category and sort by estimated item price. You can also keep a separate personal sheet using the suggested fields above.
